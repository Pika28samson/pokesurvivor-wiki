Gacha probability calculator

Your odds

Shiny results exclude collection-dependent duplicate redirection and assume the selected Charm status stays fixed. These are model estimates; your collection can improve the chance of a missing shiny.

Rules included
  • Base rarity weights: 1★ 45%, 2★ 30%, 3★ 17%, 4★ 6.5%, 5★ 1.5%. Species are equally likely within their banner rarity pool.
  • The pity counter increments before a pull. At 70, the game forces 5★; any 5★ resets that banner’s counter to zero.
  • If the first nine results in a ten-pull contain no 4★ or 5★, the tenth uses only their relative weights: 81.25% 4★ and 18.75% 5★, unless pity forces 5★.
  • A hundred-pull is ten separate ten-pulls. Normal tickets are spent in full groups of ten; one ten-pull ticket adds ten results.
  • Shiny rate is 1%, or 2% with that banner’s Shiny Charm, and requires an available shiny sprite.
  • Empty rarity pools use the game’s nearest non-empty rarity fallback. Probabilities track every pity counter and whether the current group already has a 4★+ result.
Assumptions and exclusions
  • At least one target result, not a guarantee of collecting every Pokémon. A normal target includes either form.
  • No account is read. Enter the pity counter for the selected banner; caps, currencies and collection are not imported.
  • Shiny duplicate replacement is excluded. The game redirects an already-owned shiny to a missing shiny of the same pool, or to normal if none remain.
  • Charm status is fixed throughout the calculation. Unlocking it between ten-pulls, refunds, debug-forced shinies and other ticket sources are excluded.
  • Thresholds are rounded up to complete ten-pulls and searched up to 20,000 pulls. Calculations use floating-point probabilities, not random simulation.

How the finder works
  • The shop shuffles its TM list using the date in Europe/Madrid.
  • If your partner cannot learn any of the eight base TMs, slot 8 becomes a TM chosen using that date and its Pokédex number.
  • Eligible TMs exclude Normal-type moves, moves matching either of the Pokémon’s types, and already taught moves.
  • This finder checks the final shop for every species using the game’s own code. A partner can make a TM available without being able to learn it when it is in the base stock.
  • The shop resets at midnight in Spain. Changing partners back and forth does not reroll their results.

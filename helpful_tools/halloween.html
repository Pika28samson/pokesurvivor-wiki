Candy drops: the actual odds

There is no fixed random candy-drop percentage per kill. Candy is controlled by a shared allowance that grows with elapsed run time. Each successful eligible drop gives 1 candy.

allowance = floor(elapsed seconds ÷ 60 × 1.25)
available = allowance − candy drops already created

New maps, enemies and atmosphere

Event runs cycle through three Halloween biomes instead of the normal run biomes:

  • Pumpkin Patch (Huerto de Calabazas): swamp pools, orange flowers and a dark purple palette.
  • Haunted Cemetery (Cementerio Embrujado): graveyard colours, dark water and ghostly green accents.
  • Dark Forest (Bosque Tenebroso): purple poison pools and spooky woodland.

Halloween also changes the music: tracks run at 88% tempo with a darker arrangement and added tolling bells.

After 30 minutes: the great Gengar

At 30:00, Halloween replaces Arceus’s judgment encounter with a giant Witch Gengar and Ghost typing. If you are inside a Rift arena, the judgment trigger waits until you leave it.

You receive 60 candy when the Gengar fight starts, not for defeating Gengar. The reward is added directly, outside the ordinary drop budget. Regular enemies and drops are cleared or stashed as the arena begins; collect nearby candy before the transition.

Guide: get the best event rewards

  1. Prioritise reaching 30 minutes reliably. The 60-candy encounter reward is larger than the maximum 37 ordinary drops allowed by 30:00. Use a build you can survive with; balance damage, defence and recovery.
  2. Collect steadily instead of chasing kills. Break reachable pumpkins and defeat Ghost enemies when allowance is available. Killing faster cannot bypass the time cap. Keep moving safely to find pumpkins and gather spawned candy.
  3. Pick up candy before entering judgment. A drop has already spent your allowance even if left on the floor. Aim to collect nearby drops before 30:00, without risking the run.
  4. Plan purchases around your favourites. One costume costs 300; all three cost 900. An ideal 30-minute route can yield up to 97 candy (37 collected drops + 60 encounter bonus): at least four such runs for one costume, or ten for all three, from zero candy. Actual runs can yield less, and arena delays affect timings.
  5. Spend tickets and candy independently. Save candy for costumes. Use tickets on the event banner in ten-pulls when suitable to benefit from the Epic-or-better guarantee; neither pulls nor costumes guarantee that you own the corresponding Pokémon.
  6. Buy before the shop closes. Costumes remain owned afterwards, but this shop section requires the event to be active. Do not rely on a future return or an unconfirmed candy conversion.

Damage score benchmark

Damage score imports damage_score from move-dps.csv: (actual HP damage minus remaining HP of enemies that reach the player) divided by measured seconds. The neutral benchmark uses 100 enemy HP, 15 spawns/sec and at most 25 enemies. Scores may be negative. Only maxed/evolved stages were measured. Benchmark details.
